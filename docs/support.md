# DD Dive Calculator Support & Help Center

**Last Updated:** September 27, 2026

## 🚀 Getting Started

**DD Dive Calculator** works out a dive's total score from its degree of difficulty (DD) and the judges' awards, the same way it's calculated at a meet.

1. **Enter the DD** for the dive (for example `2.4`)
2. **Enter each judge's score**: 2, 3, 5, or 7 judges
3. **Read the total**: it updates as you type
4. Tap **AC** to clear everything for the next dive

---

## 🏊 How Scoring Works

| Judges | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **2** | Both scores × 1.5 × DD |
| **3** | All three scores × DD |
| **5** | Drop the highest and lowest, then the middle three × DD |
| **7** | Drop the two highest and two lowest, then the middle three × DD |

**Example (5 judges, DD 2.4):** scores 6.5, 7, 7, 7.5, 8. Drop 6.5 and 8, so 7 + 7 + 7.5 = 21.5, and 21.5 × 2.4 = **51.60**.

Totals are shown to two decimal places. Dropped scores are shown struck through, so you can see which ones counted.

## 📱 Using the App

### Entering Numbers

- **DD** accepts values from **1.0 to 4.9** with one decimal place
- **Scores** accept **0 to 10** in half points (for example `6`, `6.5`, `10`)
- Entries **advance automatically** once complete (for example `2.4`, `7.5`, or `10`)
- **.5** adds a half point in one tap (`7` then `.5` gives `7.5`)
- **Next** moves to the following box without finishing the current one
- **Delete** (⌫) removes the last digit. If the box is empty it moves back to the previous box. Hold it to delete quickly
- **Tap any box** to jump straight to it and edit it

### The Total

- The total appears as soon as you have a DD and a valid number of scores (2, 3, 5, or 7)
- If you have entered 1, 4, or 6 scores, the app tells you what's missing instead of showing a total
- The line under the total shows the math used, for example `21.5 × 2.4`

### About Screen

Tap the **ⓘ** button at the top right to see the scoring rules, this Privacy Policy, and contact details. The app's version number is shown at the bottom of the About screen.

## ❓ Common Questions

**Why don't I see a total?**
Check that you've entered a DD and exactly 2, 3, 5, or 7 scores. With 4 or 6 scores there is no standard way to drop high and low scores, so the app waits for you to add or remove one.

**Why won't a number go in?**
The app only accepts valid entries: DD from 1.0 to 4.9, and scores from 0 to 10 in half points. For example, `7.3` or `11` can't be entered as a score. You'll feel a short vibration when an entry is rejected.

**Can I type a DD like 0.9 or 5.0?**
No. Competition DDs fall between 1.0 and 4.9, so the calculator only accepts that range.

**Does the app save my dives?**
No. The app is a quick calculator: nothing is saved or uploaded. Tap **AC** to start the next dive.

**Does the app need internet access?**
No. It works fully offline, on the pool deck or anywhere else.

**Does it work in dark mode and on iPad or tablets?**
Yes. The app follows your device's light or dark setting, and on wider screens (tablets, or phones held sideways) the keypad sits beside the scores.
